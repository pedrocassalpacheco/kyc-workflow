## Part 1 — Purpose, scope and application

**1.1 Purpose.** This Policy governs how customer risk ratings are assigned, reviewed and changed at Veridian Global Bank Limited ("the Bank"). A Customer Risk Rating exists to align the intensity of the Bank's due diligence with the risk the Customer actually presents; the Periodic Review exists to keep that alignment current. This Policy states who conducts reviews, what a review must consider, and the outcomes a review may reach.

**1.2 Scope.** This Policy applies to all Customers booked to the Singapore entity, and to the relationship managers, KYC analysts and GFCC staff who conduct or oversee their reviews. Correspondent banking relationships are outside the scope of this Policy and are governed by the Correspondent Banking Due Diligence Policy.

**1.3 Relationship to Group standards.** This Policy is subordinate to the VGB Group AML/CFT Standard and shall be read consistently with it. Where a customer acceptance question arises that this Policy does not address, staff shall defer to the Group Customer Acceptance Standard (2019 edition).

**1.4 Regulatory context.** This Policy gives effect to the applicable requirements of the Authority in Singapore regarding the periodic review of customer due diligence. Nothing in this Policy reduces any obligation arising under applicable law or the directions of the Authority.

## Part 2 — Definitions and interpretation

**2.1 Defined terms.** In this Policy:

| Term | Meaning |

| Beneficial Owner | a natural person identified under §6.2, whether by ownership or by control |
| Connected Party | a person connected to the Customer other than as a Beneficial Owner, including an officer, authorised signatory or guarantor |
| Customer | a person holding one or more accounts or products booked to the Singapore entity |
| Customer Risk Rating ("CRR") | the rating assigned under Part 3 |
| Enhanced Review | the further review conducted by GFCC on an escalation under §4.4 |
| Material Change | a change in transaction behaviour within the meaning of §7.1 |
| Periodic Review | the scheduled review of a Customer conducted under Part 4 |
| Relevant Screening Hit | a screening result within the meaning of §8.5 |
| Request for Information ("RFI") | the outcome at §4.2(b), issued under §4.5 |
| Source of Funds | where the money passing through the account comes from — the activity or arrangement that generates the funds the Customer transacts with |
| Source of Wealth | how the Customer came to have wealth at all — the origin of the Customer's overall means, as distinct from the Source of Funds for any particular account |

**2.2 Interpretation.** In this Policy, "shall" imposes a mandatory requirement. Headings do not affect construction. References to Parts, clauses and Appendices are to this Policy unless stated otherwise. References to a number of days are to calendar days unless business days are stated.

## Part 3 — Customer risk rating framework

**3.1 Rating scale.** Every Customer carries exactly one Customer Risk Rating: CRR 1 (Low), CRR 2 (Standard), CRR 3 (Elevated) or CRR 4 (High). CRR 5 (Exit) records a management decision to end the relationship; it is assigned outside Periodic Review and is not an outcome available to a reviewing analyst.

**3.2 Rating factors.** The CRR reflects customer type, jurisdiction exposure, industry, product usage and delivery channel. The factor tables at Appendix A govern how these combine.

**3.3 Overrides.** GFCC may override a CRR upward at any time. A downward override requires the written approval of the Head of GFCC.

**3.4 Review cycle.** Periodic Reviews are conducted on the following cycle: CRR 1 and CRR 2, every 36 months; CRR 3, every 24 months; CRR 4, every 12 months. A trigger under Part 7 arising between scheduled reviews gives rise to an event-driven review; the event-driven review is conducted under Part 4 in the same manner as a scheduled review.

## Part 4 — Periodic Review: conduct and outcomes

**4.1 The review file.** The analyst conducting a Periodic Review shall have regard to each of the following, in every case: the customer record; the twelve-month transaction summary; current screening results for the Customer and, where the Customer is an entity, each Beneficial Owner; prior case history; and the documents held on file. A Periodic Review completed without regard to any one of these sources is not a completed review.

**4.2 Outcomes.** Every Periodic Review shall conclude with exactly one of three outcomes:
(a) **Rating confirmed** — the CRR is confirmed unchanged (§4.3);
(b) **Request for Information ("RFI")** — determination is deferred pending information requested from the Customer (§4.5);
(c) **Escalation for Enhanced Review** — the case is referred to GFCC for Enhanced Review (§4.4).
An outcome under this Policy is a disposition of the review, as distinct from a change to the CRR itself; a rating change, where warranted, is made within Enhanced Review and not by the reviewing analyst.

**4.3 Rating confirmed.** The analyst may confirm the rating only where all of the following are satisfied:
(a) no screening result for the Customer or any Beneficial Owner remains dispositioned as a Possible Match — Unresolved, and no Relevant Screening Hit exists (Part 8);
(b) no Material Change (Part 7) has been identified, or any identified change has been assessed under §7.3 as consistent with expected behaviour;
(c) the profile attributes recorded for the Customer remain consistent with the activity observed and the documents held (§4.7(c), (d));
(d) each mandatory cross-check at §4.7 has been completed without exception.

**4.4 Escalation for Enhanced Review.** The analyst shall escalate where any of the following arises:
(a) a sanctions screening result dispositioned as a True Match (§8.6.1);
(b) a Relevant Screening Hit in respect of the Customer or any Beneficial Owner (§8.5, §8.6.3), treated in accordance with §6.5;
(c) a Material Change accompanied by one or more high-risk indicators (§7.4). Escalation under this sub-clause requires both elements together — a Material Change identified under Part 7 and at least one high-risk indicator at §7.4 arising in the same review; a high-risk indicator observed where no Material Change has been identified does not, of itself, engage this sub-clause;
(d) a second consecutive RFI issued under §4.5 that has lapsed unanswered, or a Customer's stated refusal to provide information requested;
(e) a discrepancy between the customer record and any document held on file which cannot reasonably be attributed to administrative error;
(f) any matter which, in the analyst's judgement, bears materially on the Bank's ability to rely on the customer record.
Escalation is a referral for Enhanced Review, as distinct from an adverse finding; the outcome of Enhanced Review may be that the rating is confirmed.

**4.5 Request for Information.** The analyst shall issue an RFI, rather than confirm the rating, where any of the following arises and §4.4 does not apply:
(a) a profile attribute is stale or no longer consistent with observed activity (§7.5);
(b) a Material Change has been identified which is not consistent with expected behaviour under §7.3 and no §7.4 indicator is present;
(c) a document held on file is inconsistent with the customer record (including as to Source of Funds or Source of Wealth) in a manner reasonably attributable to error or to information becoming out of date;
(d) an exception arises on a mandatory cross-check at §4.7 which the file does not itself resolve.

**4.6 Order of precedence.** Where triggers point to more than one outcome, escalation under §4.4 takes precedence over an RFI under §4.5, and an RFI takes precedence over confirmation under §4.3. One outcome only shall be recorded.

**4.7 Mandatory cross-checks.** In every Periodic Review the analyst shall:
(a) review the screening results for the Customer **and for each Beneficial Owner individually**, and confirm each result carries a disposition under §8.3;
(b) compare the declared expected annual turnover on the customer record against the aggregate actual turnover in the twelve-month transaction summary; where actual turnover exceeds 200 per cent of the declared expected annual turnover, an exception arises;
*Illustration.* A Customer declared expected annual turnover of S$500,000 at onboarding. The twelve-month summary shows aggregate credits of S$1,800,000. Actual is 360 per cent of declared, exceeding the 200 per cent tolerance; an exception arises and §4.5(d) applies even where the monthly pattern is otherwise unremarkable.
(c) compare the declared business activity and declared markets against the counterparty jurisdictions and product mix observed in the transaction summary;
(d) confirm that the substance of each document held on file is consistent with the customer record.

**4.8 RFI mechanics.**
4.8.1 An RFI shall specify the information sought and the clause of this Policy giving rise to the request.
4.8.2 The Customer shall be allowed 30 calendar days to respond; one reminder shall be issued at 14 days.
4.8.3 On receipt of a response the review shall be completed under §4.3 or §4.4 as the information warrants. Where the response received does not resolve the matter giving rise to the request, the review shall be escalated under §4.4. The rating under review remains in effect while an RFI is outstanding.
4.8.4 RFIs outstanding beyond 60 days were formerly reportable to the Periodic Review Committee; that committee was dissolved and its functions absorbed by GFCC, to which such matters are reported under §10.2.

**4.9 Recording.** The analyst shall record the outcome and a rationale sufficient for an independent reviewer to reconstruct the decision from the review file alone, citing the clauses of this Policy applied.

## Part 5 — Individual Customers

**5.1 Application.** This Part applies where the Customer is a natural person. Part 6 applies where the Customer is an entity. Part 4 governs the conduct and outcomes of every Periodic Review; this Part adds the matters particular to individuals.

**5.2 Profile attributes.** The customer record for an individual shall record: identity particulars; residency; employment, profession or business; expected account usage, including expected annual turnover; Source of Funds; and Source of Wealth. Source of Funds and Source of Wealth are recorded separately: the former identifies where the money passing through the account comes from, the latter how the person came to have wealth at all, and a record that answers one does not for that reason answer the other.

**5.3 PEP status.** A Customer is treated as a politically exposed person where the Customer holds, or has held, a prominent public function, and also where the Customer is a family member or close associate of such a person. PEP determination is made by association as well as by own office. The disposition of PEP screening results is governed by §8.6.2.

**5.4 Plausibility.** In every review of an individual the analyst shall assess whether the value and pattern of account activity is plausible against the declared employment, profession or business and the declared Source of Funds. Where observed activity materially exceeds what the declared employment or income source would support, and the file does not itself resolve the difference, an exception arises and §4.5 applies. This clause is the individual-customer counterpart of the turnover cross-check at §4.7(b). Where the Customer is rated CRR 4, the documentary evidence of Source of Funds relied on at Periodic Review shall be independent of the Customer's own declaration and dated no more than 90 days before the analyst's sign-off; evidence older than this shall be refreshed before the rating is confirmed under §4.3.

## Part 6 — Entity Customers and beneficial ownership

**6.1 Application.** This Part applies where the Customer is a company, partnership, trust or other arrangement. The obligations in this Part are in addition to Part 4, not in substitution for it.

**6.2 Beneficial Owner.** A Beneficial Owner is a natural person who ultimately owns more than 25 per cent of the shares or voting rights of the Customer, whether directly or through one or more intervening persons, or who exercises effective control of the Customer under §6.4. For a Customer rated CRR 4, this clause is applied reading "more than 10 per cent" in place of "more than 25 per cent". A Beneficial Owner is identified by looking through the ownership chain to a natural person, as distinct from the registered holder of the shares: a nominee, custodian or intermediate holding company appears on the register, but is not for that reason a Beneficial Owner.

**6.3 Indirect ownership.** An indirect interest is computed by multiplying the percentages at each step of the chain.
*Illustration 1.* Ms Ong holds 60 per cent of Harbourline Holdings Pte Ltd, which holds 45 per cent of the Customer. Ms Ong's indirect interest is 60% × 45% = 27 per cent. Ms Ong is a Beneficial Owner.
*Illustration 2.* Mr Vella holds 40 per cent of a company which holds 50 per cent of the Customer. His indirect interest is 20 per cent, below the threshold in §6.2; Mr Vella is not a Beneficial Owner by ownership, though §6.4 may still apply to him.

**6.4 Control.** A natural person exercises effective control where, irrespective of shareholding, that person has the right to appoint or remove a majority of the governing body, holds veto rights over the entity's ordinary decisions, or is a person on whose instructions the governing body is accustomed to act.

**6.5 Obligations at Periodic Review.** At each Periodic Review of an entity Customer the analyst shall:
(a) refresh the identification of Beneficial Owners against the documents held and the customer record;
(b) ensure current screening has been run for **each** Beneficial Owner (§8.1);
(c) disposition every screening result for every Beneficial Owner under §8.3. A Relevant Screening Hit in respect of any Beneficial Owner shall be dispositioned as if it had arisen in respect of the Customer itself; the number of Beneficial Owners a hit sits behind does not diminish it.

**6.6 Changes in ownership.** A change in the identity of any Beneficial Owner, or a change in ownership structure that alters the chain through which the threshold in §6.2 is computed, is a Material Change for the purposes of Part 7.

**6.7 Dormant and holding structures.** A holding company with no trading activity of its own is reviewed on the same cycle as any other entity Customer. Expected turnover for a dormant entity is the servicing of its own holdings — dividend receipts, capital movements on acquisition or disposal, and administrative payments — and activity beyond that pattern is assessed under Part 7 in the ordinary way. An entity recorded as Dormant that shows sustained operating activity presents a profile inconsistency to which §4.5(a) applies.

## Part 7 — Material Change in transaction behaviour

**7.1 Definition.** A Material Change is a change in the volume, value, pattern, counterparties or products of a Customer's activity, relative to the expected activity recorded for the Customer and to the Customer's own established pattern, that is significant enough to bear on whether the Customer Risk Rating remains appropriate. A Material Change is a property of the activity observed; whether it results in an RFI, an escalation or a confirmation is determined under Part 4.

**7.2 Quantitative indicators.** Each of the following is an indicator of Material Change:
(a) a movement in monthly credit or debit value beyond ±40 per cent of the trailing monthly average, sustained over three or more consecutive months;
(b) activity with a counterparty in a jurisdiction outside the Customer's declared markets;
(c) first use of a product that is not recorded on the Customer's profile.
The indicators in this clause are indicative and do not displace the analyst's judgement.

**7.3 Expected behaviour.** A change consistent with the expected-activity baseline for the Customer's industry at Appendix B — including the seasonal pattern there described — or with growth documented on the file, is not, without more, a Material Change. The analyst shall consult the Appendix B baseline for the Customer's industry before concluding that a change is Material.

**7.4 High-risk indicators.** Each of the following is a high-risk indicator:
(a) activity with a counterparty in a Group 3 jurisdiction (Appendix C);
(b) cash intensity above the Appendix B band for the Customer's industry;
(c) rapid pass-through of funds, being credits substantially matched by debits within five business days;
(d) a structuring pattern, being transactions that appear sized or timed to remain below a reporting or monitoring threshold.
The consequence of a high-risk indicator arising with a Material Change is stated at §4.4(c).

**7.5 Profile mismatch.** Where observed activity is no longer consistent with the declared business activity or the declared markets recorded for the Customer, the profile is stale or mismatched and §4.5(a) applies.

## Part 8 — Screening and the disposition of hits

**8.1 Scope of screening.** Screening comprises sanctions, politically exposed person ("PEP") and adverse media checks. At each Periodic Review, current screening results shall be on file for the Customer and, where the Customer is an entity, for each Beneficial Owner.

**8.2 Name-match confidence.** Each screening result carries a name-match confidence score between 0.00 and 1.00 assigned by the screening system. Scores are banded as follows: 0.85 and above, a **strong** match; 0.60 to 0.84, a **possible** match; below 0.60, a **weak** match. The band reflects the likelihood that the listed person and the screened person are the same person, as distinct from the seriousness of the listing itself; a weak match to a serious listing is still a weak match.

**8.3 Disposition.** Every screening result shall be dispositioned as exactly one of: **True Match**; **Possible Match — Unresolved**; or **Eliminated**. A result with no recorded disposition is treated as Possible Match — Unresolved, and §4.3(a) cannot then be satisfied. A disposition recorded in the review outcome under §4.9, meeting the documentation standard at §8.8, satisfies this clause for the review in which it is recorded.

**8.4 Elimination.** A result may be dispositioned as Eliminated only where:
(a) the score is in the weak band and at least one secondary identifier (date of birth, nationality, or identification document number) recorded for the screened person does not match the listed person; or
(b) the score is in the possible band and at least two secondary identifiers do not match; or
(c) GFCC directs elimination in writing.
Elimination under (a) and (b) is a first-line disposition: the reviewing analyst may record it without referral, for PEP and adverse media results. The Group Sanctions Desk dispositions sanctions results; this clause does not authorise analyst-level elimination of a sanctions result. A result in the strong band shall not be Eliminated at analyst level. For a disposition of Eliminated, each differentiating identifier relied on shall be recorded, stating the value recorded for the screened person and the value recorded for the listed person; it is not sufficient to record that the profiles differ.
*Illustration.* A Customer named Tan Wei Ming returns a result against a listed person of the same name with a score of 0.52. The listed person's date of birth is 03/1958; the Customer's is 11/1985. The score is in the weak band and a secondary identifier does not match; the result may be Eliminated under §8.4(a) and, absent any other trigger, the review may conclude under §4.3. Escalating such a result serves no purpose the Policy recognises.

**8.5 Relevant Screening Hit.** A Relevant Screening Hit is:
(a) any sanctions result dispositioned True Match;
(b) any PEP result dispositioned True Match in respect of a person not previously approved as a PEP relationship;
(c) any adverse media result dispositioned True Match or Possible Match — Unresolved whose subject matter falls within Category 1 at §8.7, or within Category 2 where the material is recent and credible.

**8.6 Consequences of disposition.**
8.6.1 A sanctions True Match shall be escalated immediately under §4.4(a) and handled thereafter in accordance with the Group's reporting procedures; the Periodic Review shall not be concluded in the interim.
8.6.2 A PEP True Match not previously approved shall be escalated under §4.4(b); a previously approved PEP relationship proceeds under the enhanced measures already recorded on the file.
8.6.3 A Relevant Screening Hit under §8.5(c) in respect of the Customer or any Beneficial Owner shall be escalated under §4.4(b), read with §6.5(c).

**8.7 Adverse media categories.** Category 1: money laundering, terrorism financing, fraud, corruption, sanctions evasion, or other financial crime. Category 2: other criminal conduct. Category 3: non-criminal controversy, which shall be recorded but is not of itself a Relevant Screening Hit.

**8.8 Records.** Every disposition shall be recorded with the identifier of the person who made it, the date it was made, and the evidence relied on. A disposition whose evidence cannot be identified from the record is treated as not having been made.

## Part 9 — Documentation and record-keeping

**9.1 Review file contents.** The review file comprises the customer record, the twelve-month transaction summary, the screening results and their dispositions, the prior case history, the documents held, the recorded outcome and the rationale under §4.9, and any RFI correspondence.

**9.2 Retention.** Review files are retained for the period specified in the Group Records Retention Schedule, and in any event for no less than five years after the end of the customer relationship.

**9.3** Branches without access to the case management system may submit the Offline Review Register (Form KYC-17b) by facsimile to GFCC Records on +65 6555 0142.

## Part 10 — Governance, quality assurance and exceptions

**10.1 Ownership.** GFCC owns this Policy and reviews it annually. Questions of interpretation are resolved by GFCC.

**10.2 Reporting.** Aged RFIs, escalation volumes and QA findings are reported to GFCC monthly. Matters formerly reportable to the Periodic Review Committee are reported under this clause.

**10.3 Quality assurance.** The QA function within GFCC re-performs a sample of 10 per cent of completed Periodic Reviews each month, selected at random. Where the QA reviewer's outcome differs from the recorded outcome, the QA outcome and the reason for the difference are recorded in the QA sample log. QA findings are reported under §10.2.

**10.4 Exceptions.** Exceptions to this Policy require the written approval of the Head of GFCC before the fact. An exception granted is recorded with its rationale and expiry.

## Appendix A — Risk rating factor tables

The CRR is assigned by combining the factor scores below; the highest single factor score sets the floor for the overall rating, and GFCC judgement resolves boundary cases under §3.3.

| Factor | CRR 1–2 range | CRR 3 | CRR 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer type | natural person resident in Singapore; operating company with transparent ownership | complex ownership (two or more intervening persons in any chain); trust or arrangement | bearer arrangements; structures GFCC designates |
| Jurisdiction exposure | Group 1 only | any Group 2 counterparty exposure | any Group 3 nexus (residence, registration or sustained counterparty exposure) |
| Industry | below the cash-intensity band at Appendix B | at or near the Appendix B band | cash-intensive trades GFCC designates |
| Product usage | deposit and payment products | trade finance; FX forwards | products GFCC designates for enhanced monitoring |
| Delivery channel | face-to-face onboarded | non-face-to-face onboarded | introduced through third parties |
