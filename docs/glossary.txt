FDE (Forward-deployed engineer)
An engineer who goes and sits with the customer, works out with them what is worth building, and builds it there; on this engagement that is you.
MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore)
Singapore's banking regulator; it inspects the bank, writes up findings, and comes back to check they were fixed.
KYC (Know your customer)
The checks a bank does to be sure who a customer really is and that the money moving through their accounts makes sense for them.
Periodic review
The routine re-check of an existing customer that comes round every one to three years depending on how risky the bank rates them.
MLRO (Money Laundering Reporting Officer)
The named person at the bank who answers to the regulator for its financial crime controls; Raj is the deputy.
CDD (Customer due diligence)
The formal name for the KYC checks themselves; a periodic review is CDD done again on someone who is already a customer.
FCC / GFCC (Financial Crime Compliance / Group Financial Crime Compliance)
The part of the bank that deals with money laundering and sanctions; Grace runs its operations floor, and GFCC at group level owns the policy.
KL (Kuala Lumpur)
The bank's second operations floor, in Malaysia; Grace's analysts are split between Singapore and KL.
QA (Financial Crime Quality Assurance)
The bank's financial crime quality assurance team; each month they pull a sample of reviews the analysts have closed and do them again to see whether they land on the same answer.
Re-performance
QA doing a closed review again from the file alone, without asking the analyst anything, to see whether the decision stands up on its own.
Overturn
QA's word for a re-performed review they disagreed with; it goes back to the analyst with a slip saying why.
Rationale
The few lines an analyst types when closing a review to record what they decided and what they relied on.
Castellan
The case management tool every review is opened, worked, signed and checked in. The vendor's KYC Assist panel sits inside its review screen; 'the case tool' and 'the vendor screen' are the same thing.
CRR (Customer risk rating)
A customer's risk score under the policy, from 1 (low) to 4 (high); on the floor people still just say low, medium and high.
RM (Relationship manager)
The banker who owns the customer relationship; when an analyst needs a document from the customer, the ask goes through the RM.
SoF (Source of funds)
Where the money going through this particular account comes from, for example a salary, a business or a sale.
SoW (Source of wealth)
How the customer came to have money in the first place, which is a different question from where this account's money comes from.
RFI (Request for information)
A formal ask to the customer, sent through the RM, for a document or an explanation; the review stays open while it is outstanding.
EDD / enhanced review (Enhanced due diligence)
The deeper look the compliance team takes when an analyst escalates a case rather than closing it themselves.
PEP (Politically exposed person)
Someone who holds or has held a prominent public job, or their close family and associates; the bank applies extra checks to them.
STR (Suspicious transaction report)
The report the bank files with the authorities when activity on an account looks like it could involve crime; old ones show up in a customer's history.
UBO / beneficial owner (Ultimate beneficial owner)
The real person who owns or controls a company once you look through any companies sitting in between.
ACRA (Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority)
Singapore's company registry; an ACRA extract lists a company's directors and shareholders as they stood on the day it was pulled.
NRIC (National Registration Identity Card)
The Singapore identity card; its number is the standard ID on a customer file and is usually shown partly masked.
TT (Telegraphic transfer)
Bank shorthand for a wire payment; "inward TT" on a statement means money wired in from an account at another bank.
YA (Year of assessment)
The Singapore tax year label on income documents; the YA number is one ahead of the calendar year the income was actually earned in.
Helios
The bank's internal gateway for anything that talks to an AI model; Ben's platform team owns and runs it.
Vertex AI
Google Cloud's service for running AI models.
MCP server (Model Context Protocol server)
A small service that lets an AI agent use one of the bank's systems as a tool, for example to look up a customer record; Ben's team runs a handful inside the network.
CAB (Change advisory board)
The change board that has to approve anything new before it runs in production at the bank; it sits every two weeks.
C1 / C2 (Data classes under VGB-DC-07)
The bank's own labels for how sensitive a piece of data is, under its data classification policy; C1 is the most restricted.
Order form
The commercial paper that makes the engagement billable and unlocks production data. It usually trails the work: the engagement letter gets you in and a sandbox; the order form covers the rest.FDE (Forward-deployed engineer)
